About Dream Resort & Club, Ashta

Dream Resort & Club, Ashta is a premium wedding destination and luxury resort strategically located between Indore and Bhopal Highway.

Operational since 2021, Dream Resort & Club has successfully hosted more than 60 grand weddings along with numerous corporate events, parties and private celebrations.

With 32 luxury rooms, a spacious wedding lawn, elegant banquet hall, swimming pool and upcoming private pool & jacuzzi rooms, we offer a complete destination experience for weddings, family stays and memorable events.
